讓臨床 AI 從病歷資料走到病床邊：用嚴謹的驗證、清楚的法規路徑與真實場域合作，把研究變成臨床可用的工具。

Moving clinical AI from health records to the bedside, through rigorous validation, clear regulatory pathways, and real-world partnerships.

我是醫學資訊研究者，研究聚焦於醫療人工智慧、慢性腎臟病的數位健康介入，以及醫療軟體（SaMD）的臨床驗證與法規策略。研究以臺北醫學大學臨床研究資料庫（TMUCRD）為基礎，涵蓋北醫附醫、萬芳、雙和三院的長期電子病歷資料。

I am a medical informatics researcher working on clinical AI, digital health interventions for chronic kidney disease, and the validation and regulatory strategy of software as a medical device (SaMD). Much of my work draws on the TMU Clinical Research Database (TMUCRD), which links longitudinal EHR data across three affiliated hospitals: TMU Hospital, Wan Fang, and Shuang Ho.

研究主題ResearchResearch themes

臨床 AI 驗證方法學

Clinical AI validation

以多院區電子病歷建立與外部驗證預測模型，比較傳統機器學習與新一代表格基礎模型的表現，並研究大型語言模型在臨床文件中的錯誤偵測與安全性。

Building and externally validating prediction models on multi-site EHR data, benchmarking classical machine learning against newer tabular foundation models, and studying error detection and safety of large language models in clinical documentation.

Prediction modelsExternal validationLLM safety

慢性腎臟病數位健康介入

Digital health for CKD

針對血液透析與慢性腎臟病族群，設計並以隨機對照試驗評估數位介入，包括透析中運動、穿戴式生理量測，以及生成式 AI 衛教的臨床效能與成本效益。

Designing and testing digital interventions for hemodialysis and CKD populations in randomized trials, including intradialytic exercise, wearable physiological monitoring, and the clinical effectiveness and cost-effectiveness of generative-AI patient education.

HemodialysisRCTWearablesGenAI education

SaMD 法規與臨床轉譯

SaMD regulation & translation

協助醫療 AI 產品規劃 TFDA / FDA 法規路徑、臨床決策支援（CDS）定位、可用性工程（IEC 62366）與 IRB 研究設計，讓產品在上市前累積可信的臨床證據。

Planning TFDA and FDA pathways for medical AI products, clinical decision support (CDS) positioning, usability engineering under IEC 62366, and IRB study design, so products build credible clinical evidence before market entry.

TFDAFDA CDSIEC 62366IRB

醫療大數據分析與視覺化

Health big-data analytics

運用世代研究、病例對照研究與統合分析，探討藥物暴露與癌症、心血管及神經退化疾病風險，並以視覺化方法呈現複雜的臨床資料。

Cohort, case-control, and meta-analytic studies of drug exposure and the risk of cancer, cardiovascular, and neurodegenerative disease, with visual analytics for complex clinical data.

PharmacoepidemiologyCohort studyMeta-analysis
